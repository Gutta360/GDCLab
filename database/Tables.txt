Tables in schema gdclab:

patients             Person master; mobile mandatory, address optional, age 0..120.
branches             Branch details used for bill headers.
referral_sources     Shared Doctor/Lab/Self/Other master.
test_categories      Extensible test categories.
diagnostic_tests     Current Test Master, initially 41 tests.
registrations        One visit per record; independent Doctor and Lab referrals.
registration_tests   Visit tests, historical prices/categories, execution lifecycle.
payments             Multiple confirmed collections per registration.
